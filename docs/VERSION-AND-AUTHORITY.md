# Version and Authority

## Two clocks are running

CJIS assessments operate on at least two clocks:

1. the policy the FBI has published; and
2. the baseline the applicable authority currently audits or sanctions.

They can differ during a transition.

FBI CJIS Security Policy v6.1 is dated June 25, 2026. The policy identifies existing and Priority 1 requirements as sanctionable and places Priority 2-4 modernized requirements in a zero-cycle ending September 30, 2027.

That federal transition does not erase jurisdictional implementation instructions. Texas DPS, for example, states that Texas audits use v5.9.5 through March 31, 2027 while v6.1 is used for gap assessment and readiness.

The lesson is not “Texas is the rule.”

The lesson is: ask your authority which clock governs which decision.

## Authority map

The FBI CJIS ISO maintains and distributes the policy and supplies technical guidance. The CJIS Advisory Process develops policy recommendations. The CSA provides statewide or equivalent CJIS service. The CSO administers the CJIS network, approves access, and carries jurisdictional responsibility defined by policy and agreements. Agency personnel implement and operate the controls. Contractors operate only inside the authority and agreements granted to them.

For a specific assessment, replace every institutional noun with a real person, office, or record.

“The business approved it” is not a CJIS authority record.

## Authority questions

Ask the applicable official:

1. Which policy version is currently audited?
2. Which v6.1 priorities are currently sanctionable here?
3. Which state or local supplements apply?
4. Which agreements and addenda are required for this relationship?
5. Which personnel require screening, training, testing, or certification?
6. How does the jurisdiction interpret access to encrypted CJI and administrative capability?
7. Which cloud/provider evidence is acceptable?
8. Are compensating controls permitted, and who approves them?
9. What event triggers reassessment?
10. What written record constitutes acceptance?

Record answers. Do not paraphrase consequential exceptions from memory.
