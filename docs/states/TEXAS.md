# Texas CJIS requirements

**Reviewed November 19, 2026.** The Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) is the CJIS Systems Agency for Texas. It adopts the FBI CJIS Security Policy and adds four Texas requirements. DPS says all Texas CJIS audits through **March 31, 2027** use **v5.9.5**. It directs agencies to use **v6.1** for readiness and gap assessments. Its published notice leaves the audit edition after that date open. [DPS policy and audit editions](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/crime-records/cjis-documents) · [Texas supplement](https://www.dps.texas.gov/sites/default/files/documents/securityreview/texascjissecuritypolicy.pdf)

[Searchable requirements](../../data/state-search-pilot.json) · [November source register](../../sources/manifests/texas-2026-11-19.md) · [September DPS collection](../../sources/manifests/texas-dps-cjis-documents.md)

## Which Texas rules apply?

| Situation | Start here |
|---|---|
| A police department buys a hosted records system | Trace which services and people can process, store, transmit or see CJI. DPS asks agencies to identify applications with direct **and indirect** access. Material copied from a protected TLETS return can bring a local report or receiving system into audit scope. DPS treats driver-license and vehicle-registration returns separately. [DPS technical audit, pp. 1–3](https://www.dps.texas.gov/sites/default/files/documents/securityreview/documents/cjis_technical_audit_5.9.5_v2.pdf#page=1) |
| A county IT department or private vendor supports a justice agency | Determine who exercises management control and which staff can access CJI. DPS provides different agreements for government dispatch or IT support and private contractors. For criminal history record information (CHRI), Government Code §411.083 also specifies contract terms and authorized purposes. [DPS agreement templates](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/crime-records/sample-agreements-policy-templates) · [§411.083](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/docs/GV/htm/GV.411.htm#411.083) |
| A school, licensing board or other noncriminal justice agency screens applicants | Identify the statute authorizing that recipient and population, then check DPS Secure Site and FACT program terms. Texas-only name searches, fingerprint results and subscriptions have different coverage and eligibility. [DPS Secure Site policy](https://crimerecords.dps.texas.gov/DpsWebsite/CriminalHistory/SecurityPolicy.aspx) · [FACT Clearinghouse guide, pp. 5–7](https://crimerecords.dps.texas.gov/Clearinghouse/Content/FACT%20Clearinghouse%20User%20Guide%202026.pdf#page=5) |
| Court personnel review or run a criminal history in the Public Safety Report System (PSRS) | The Office of Court Administration (OCA) specifies annual awareness training for every user. Users who personally generate criminal histories also need TCIC mobile-access certification, renewed every two years. A judge who only reviews another authorized user's report follows the view-only requirements. [OCA training table](https://www.txcourts.gov/bail/mandatory-training-requirements/) |

## The four requirements in the Texas supplement

| Topic | Texas requirement |
|---|---|
| Physical security | A physically secure location needs sufficient **physical and personnel controls**. DPS includes an enclosed police vehicle among the locations that can qualify. |
| Third-party cloud hosting | When an agency proposes a third-party hosted service to process, store or transmit CJI, the **deployment environment** must be FedRAMP authorized at **Moderate or High**. Identify the actual service and boundary in the assessment. |
| Offshore personnel | DPS bars an agency from granting offshore resources operating outside the identified APB-member boundaries unescorted logical access enabling them to view, change or use **unencrypted CJI** outside those boundaries. The supplement identifies the United States, U.S. territories, Indian tribes and Canada. Document support-staff location, access privileges and where plaintext can be used. |
| End-of-life equipment | Replace IT systems with CJIS connectivity **within six months** of end of life or loss of manufacturer support for security fixes, updates and patches. |

All four provisions appear on the single-page state supplement revised May 15, 2025. [Texas DPS supplement, p. 1](https://www.dps.texas.gov/sites/default/files/documents/securityreview/texascjissecuritypolicy.pdf)

**State procurement can add TX-RAMP.** The contracting state agency determines whether a cloud service is in scope and the applicable level. The program uses Level 1 for low-impact resources and Level 2 for moderate- or high-impact resources. The manual recognizes qualifying FedRAMP authorization at the corresponding level and gives provisional designations different treatment. Check the service's actual designation and what the agency is buying. A Texas political subdivision's use of CJI alone establishes neither state-agency procurement status nor a particular TX-RAMP level. [TX-RAMP manual v4.0, §§4–9](https://dir.texas.gov/sites/default/files/2026-02/TX-RAMP%20Program%20Manual%204.0.pdf#page=7)

The February 2026 manual uses the rule numbers then in force. Effective **August 1, 2026**, the Texas Administrative Code moved the information security standards into **1 TAC Chapter 477** under Texas Cyber Command. The transfer table maps TX-RAMP minimum standards to §477.5, state agencies to §477.27 and higher education to §477.77. [Texas Register notice](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/June262026/Transferred%20Rules/Transferred%20Rules.html) · [Official crosswalk, pp. 1–2](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/June262026/Transferred%20Rules/202602469-1.pdf)

## Contractor access and screening

- **Contract and people.** The DPS audit asks for the signed contractor addendum and individual acknowledgments for personnel with CJI access. Texas permits signing its signatory page or incorporating the addendum by reference and furnishing DPS a copy of the contract. Document covered subcontractors, individual screening, authorization and roster reviews. For government IT or dispatch support, document the criminal justice agency's management control under the appropriate agreement. [Addendum, cover and Texas signatory page](https://www.dps.texas.gov/sites/default/files/documents/securityreview/documents/fbiSecurityAddendum.pdf) · [Technical audit, pp. 4–6](https://www.dps.texas.gov/sites/default/files/documents/securityreview/documents/cjis_technical_audit_5.9.5_v2.pdf#page=4)
- **Each agency adjudicates access.** The October 13, 2026 chart calls for adjudication each time an agency employs or contracts with an individual. It includes personnel such as custodians who may enter areas holding CJI. Read the applicant and existing-user columns separately. For an open felony or misdemeanor arrest, the applicant waits for disposition while an existing user maintains access pending disposition. The chart also addresses active TCOLE peace, communications and corrections officer licenses, agency-specific waivers, and set-aside dispositions. The index still labels the chart March 31, 2026. Its linked PDF carries the October revision. [Current DPS chart](https://www.dps.texas.gov/sites/default/files/documents/securityreview/tx_cjis_access_policy.pdf) · [DPS document listing](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/crime-records/cjis-documents)
- **Houston adds local paperwork.** HPD requires individual certification for staff working under an addendum. A contractor employee seeking remote access also submits Houston's remote security clearance and logical system access forms and reviews the remote-access terms. Select Houston as the customer when searching for those forms. [Houston Police vendor instructions](https://www.houstontx.gov/police/cjis/hpdvendorcertification.htm)
- **State contractor cyber training.** A state contract giving the contractor access to a state computer system or database must include certified cybersecurity training. Covered contractors, including subcontractors, complete it during the contract and each renewal and verify completion. The agency reports by August 31 annually. This obligation has its own trigger alongside CJIS role-based training. [Gov’t Code §2063.104](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/GV/htm/GV.2063.htm#2063.104)

## Criminal history and fingerprint checks

CHRI held by DPS is generally confidential. Chapter 411 identifies who may receive it and for which purpose. It provides for criminal justice access and specified noncriminal justice uses. For a service contract covered by §411.083(b)(5) or (6), the agreement must authorize access, limit use to its stated purpose, protect security and confidentiality, specify sanctions, and require the provider to follow DPS instructions. A downstream vendor handling checks for a noncriminal justice agency also needs **prior DPS permission and a third-party Outsourcing Agreement** under the FACT guide. [Gov’t Code §§411.082–.085](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/docs/GV/htm/GV.411.htm#411.083) · [FACT guide, p. 7](https://crimerecords.dps.texas.gov/Clearinghouse/Content/FACT%20Clearinghouse%20User%20Guide%202026.pdf#page=7)

| Decision | Texas answer |
|---|---|
| Public search or authorized screening? | Section 411.135 describes the DPS information available to the public, including qualifying convictions and deferred adjudications, subject to statutory exclusions. Restricted searches and fingerprint subscriptions require the recipient's own authority. Confirm identity before acting on a name match. [§411.135](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/docs/GV/htm/GV.411.htm#411.135) · [DPS Secure Site policy](https://crimerecords.dps.texas.gov/DpsWebsite/CriminalHistory/SecurityPolicy.aspx) |
| When should a FACT subscription end? | The January 2026 FACT guide directs the subscriber to unsubscribe immediately upon loss of the qualifying relationship, including a decision not to hire. Secure Site policy §IV.C specifies three business days from the end of association. Section 411.0845(g) requires notice as soon as practicable when entitlement ends. Immediate offboarding satisfies all three. The statute's separate 48-hour clock runs from **DPS becoming aware of a change** to the record. [FACT guide, p. 6](https://crimerecords.dps.texas.gov/Clearinghouse/Content/FACT%20Clearinghouse%20User%20Guide%202026.pdf#page=6) · [Secure Site policy](https://crimerecords.dps.texas.gov/DpsWebsite/CriminalHistory/SecurityPolicy.aspx) · [§411.0845(f)–(g)](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/docs/GV/htm/GV.411.htm#411.0845) |
| How are errors or court orders handled? | A person can request their own DPS history and challenge inaccuracies with the Error Resolution Unit. Under §411.075, the clerk generally sends a nondisclosure order to DPS within 15 business days of issuance, DPS seals within 10 business days of receipt, and notified Texas entities seal within 30 business days of receipt. Statutory exceptions govern later disclosures. Expunction has its own procedures under Code of Criminal Procedure Chapter 55A, effective January 1, 2025. [DPS error resolution](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/crime-records/criminal-history-error-resolution) · [§411.075](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/docs/GV/htm/GV.411.htm#411.075) · [Gov’t Code, Subchapter E-1](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/docs/GV/htm/GV.411.htm#411.071) · [Chapter 55A](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/CR/htm/CR.55A.htm) |

## Security incidents and breach notices

A county sheriff discovering a compromise of a TLETS-connected system should evaluate each row below. Different facts start each clock. Use the current contact details on the issuing office's page or form.

| Trigger | Recipient and deadline |
|---|---|
| Confirmed security incident involving TLETS or CJIS | DPS directs staff to alert their agency security lead immediately, then notify the 24/7 DPS Enterprise Technology Operations Center (ETOC), **512-424-2139** or **888-377-6420**. DPS gives **one hour from discovery**. Complete the CSIRC written form and send it to the committee and copy the ISO/CSO as instructed on the form. The reporting page cites v6.0 for its timing. The separate Texas audit notice names v5.9.5. Secure Site §V.A also calls for a mobile-device incident plan and reporting incidents to DPS on its form. [Secure Site policy, §V.A](https://crimerecords.dps.texas.gov/DpsWebsite/CriminalHistory/SecurityPolicy.aspx) · [DPS incident instructions](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/crime-records/welcome-tx-cjis-security-office) · [DPS incident form](https://www.dps.texas.gov/sites/default/files/documents/securityreview/documents/incidentResponseForm.docx) |
| Cybersecurity incident at a state agency or local government covered by §2063.302 | Within **48 hours of discovery**, notify Texas Cyber Command including its chief, or the Secretary of State for an incident involving election data. State agencies and higher education use SPECTRIM. Local governments use the local incident form. The Command accepts the initial report at **877-347-2476**. The follow-up with cause analysis is due to the Command by the **10th business day after eradication, closure and recovery**. Subsection (c) has a local utility-reporting exception. [§2063.302](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/GV/htm/GV.2063.htm#2063.302) · [Texas Cyber Command reporting routes](https://www.txcc.texas.gov/report-an-incident) |
| Breach of sensitive personal information meeting §521.053 | A covered owner or licensee notifies affected individuals without unreasonable delay and within **60 days of determining a breach occurred**, subject to the statutory conditions and delay provisions. At **250 or more Texas residents**, notify the Attorney General electronically as soon as practicable and within **30 days of determination**. A qualifying data maintainer must notify the owner or licensee immediately after discovery. Section 2063.302(a)(1) also applies these notice duties to covered public entities. [§521.053](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/BC/htm/BC.521.htm#521.053) · [Attorney General breach reporting](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/data-breach-reporting) |

## Reporting, retention and audits

| Record or program | What the authority requires |
|---|---|
| Adult criminal-history reporting | Start the incident fingerprint record for a felony, jailable misdemeanor or fine-only family-violence misdemeanor. Report the arrest within **seven days**. The clerk reports the disposition. The default for other Chapter 66 data is prompt reporting within **30 days of receipt**, subject to exceptions. Electronic arrest reports require associated fingerprints. Agencies may enter dispositions manually or send batches, including through an RMS/JMS vendor. [Art. 66.252](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/CR/htm/CR.66.htm#66.252) · [Electronic Arrest Reporting](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/crime-records/electronic-arrest-reporting-ear) · [Electronic Disposition Reporting](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/crime-records/electronic-disposition-reporting-edr) |
| Juvenile records | Family Code §58.110 has its own fingerprint, seven-day custody and general 30-day reporting provisions. For covered juvenile law enforcement records kept electronically in the same system as adult records, §58.008 requires separate access controls. Section 58.106 identifies recipients of confidential state juvenile-system information. [§58.008](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/docs/FA/htm/FA.58.htm#58.008) · [§58.106](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/docs/FA/htm/FA.58.htm#58.106) · [§58.110](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/docs/FA/htm/FA.58.htm#58.110) |
| Local criminal-history logs | Texas State Library and Archives Commission Local Schedule PS4175-05a sets a **three-year minimum** for records documenting dissemination of criminal histories. PS4175-05b assigns administrative-value retention to the qualifying inquiry, receipt and message records it describes. Classify the actual record and honor holds, case-file rules and longer applicable periods. [Local Schedule PS, series PS4175-05a–b](https://www.tsl.texas.gov/slrm/localretention/schedule_ps) |
| License plate readers at two local agencies | The **Bexar County Sheriff** publishes 30 days for Flock data and indefinite retention of the user-access audit trail. **San Marcos** records that all city-contracted Flock cameras were deactivated and removed by February 1, 2026. Select the locality and date when comparing these policies. [Bexar County Sheriff's policy](https://www.bexar.org/4072/Flock-Safety) · [San Marcos program history](https://www.sanmarcostx.gov/4530/Automated-License-Plate-Readers-ALPR) |
| Audit scope and evidence | The DPS technical checklist asks for agreements, personnel and access rosters, incident and audit records, network diagrams, inventories and media procedures. The TCIC program describes a **three-year** local audit cycle. Article 66.352 orders the state auditor to examine statewide record accuracy, completeness and reporting timeliness at least once in **five years**. Article 66.353 separately requires annual county completeness figures. [Technical audit, p. 68](https://www.dps.texas.gov/sites/default/files/documents/securityreview/documents/cjis_technical_audit_5.9.5_v2.pdf#page=68) · [DPS services catalog, TCIC Audit](https://www.dps.texas.gov/sites/default/files/documents/administration/crime_records/training/catb_services_catalogue.pdf) · [Arts. 66.352–.353](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/CR/htm/CR.66.htm#66.352) |

## Search Texas

From the repository root, change the topic to `vendor-access`, `vendor-screening`, `incident-reporting`, `records-retention`, `training` or `policy-version`. Each result includes its trigger and source. A locality identifies a city or county example.

```sh
jq -r --arg topic 'cloud-hosting' '
  . as $guide | (.records[], .coverage_gaps[])
  | select(.jurisdiction == "US-TX" and
      (.topic == $topic or ((.related_topics // []) | index($topic) != null)))
  | [(.kind // .status), (.locality // ""), (.applies_when // ""),
     (.summary // .detail), (.locator // ""), (.url // $guide.sources[.source].url)] | @tsv
' data/state-search-pilot.json
```

**Two publication conflicts matter for training.** The live DPS Secure Site policy requires awareness training before access and annually. An older DPS PDF still gives a later initial deadline and a biennial interval. OCA's May 2026 PSRS guide also mixes annual and biennial awareness language, while its live role table specifies annual awareness and biennial mobile-access certification. Use the program's current instructions for its users and record which course a certificate covers.
