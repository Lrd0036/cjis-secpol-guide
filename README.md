# CJIS Security Policy Guide (CJISSECPOL v6.0)

Practical guide to understanding FBI CJIS Security Policy. The official policy is 400+ pages and written in dense, hard-to-navigate jargon. 

I am aware that CJIS is largely different from other frameworks due to the nature of it being governed by the FBI. Simply put: FBI decides if it's CJIS compliant or not. 

It's not just NIST. While CJIS may be largely based off NIST SP 800-53, ultimately it adds significant mandatory operational and legal requirements & roles. 

## Disclaimer
This guide is an unofficial community resources for educational purposes and is not endorsed by the FBI or any State Identification Bureau.

Always consult your local CJIS Systems Officer (CSO) for official compliance rulings and guidelines.
