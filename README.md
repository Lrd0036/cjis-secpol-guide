# CJIS Security Policy Guide (CJISSECPOL v6.0)

Practical guide to understanding FBI CJIS Security Policy. The official policy is 400+ pages and written in dense, hard-to-navigate jargon. 

I am aware that CJIS is largely different from other frameworks due to the nature of it being governed by the FBI. Simply put: **there is no central FBI certification. Instead, individual state CJIS Systems Agencies (CSAs) and local CJIS Systems Officers (CSOs) hold the ultimate authority on audits and deciding if a vendor is CJIS compliant.**

**In other words, don't make your CSO or your CSA angry.**

It's not just NIST. While CJIS may be largely based off NIST SP 800-53, ultimately it adds significant mandatory operational and legal requirements & roles... including mandatory CJIS security addendums for private contractors, fingerprint-based national background checks, and specific local officer roles–CSOs, LASOs, and TACs.

## Disclaimer
This guide is an unofficial community resources for educational purposes and is not endorsed by the FBI or any State Identification Bureau.

Always consult your local CJIS Systems Officer (CSO) for official compliance rulings and guidelines.
