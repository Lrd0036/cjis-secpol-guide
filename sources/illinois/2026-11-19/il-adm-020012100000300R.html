CHAPTER II: ILLINOIS STATE POLICE
PART 1210 INDIVIDUAL'S RIGHT TO ACCESS AND REVIEW CRIMINAL HISTORY RECORD INFORMATION
SECTION 1210.30 FEE

 

Section 1210.30  Fee

 

The facility, licensed fingerprint vendor, or licensed fingerprint vendor agency of which the request is made may charge a fee of up to the actual cost of processing the request.  This fee may be waived.

 

(Source:  Amended at 39 Ill. Reg. 3431, effective February 23, 2015)
