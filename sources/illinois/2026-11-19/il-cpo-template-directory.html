Scam Alert: Fake Emails Impersonating CPO-GS

Be alert for fraudulent emails attempting to steal your banking information, login credentials, credit card details, or attempting to request payments. The Chief Procurement Office for General Services (CPO-GS) will never request financial information or payments via email or text message and only in limited circumstances request personal information. To confirm official communications and legitimate emails from the CPO-GS, email cpo.gs@illinois.gov.
