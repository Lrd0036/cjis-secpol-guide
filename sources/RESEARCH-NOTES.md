# Research Notes and Sources

## Evidence classes

Sources in this repository are separated by what they can establish.

### Governing and primary

- [FBI CJIS Security Policy Resource Center](https://le.fbi.gov/cjis-division/cjis-security-policy-resource-center)
- [FBI Requirements Companion Document for CJIS Security Policy v6.1](https://le.fbi.gov/cjis-division/cjis-security-policy-resource-center/requirement-companion-document-pdf)
- [FBI CJIS Security Policy appendices](https://le.fbi.gov/cjis-division/cjis-security-policy-resource-center/appendicies)
- [FBI Criminal Justice Information Services](https://www.fbi.gov/services/cjis)
- [28 CFR Part 20](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/chapter-I/part-20)
- Applicable Compact Council rules, CJIS system manuals, agency agreements, and jurisdictional supplements

These establish policy, definitions, legal restrictions, responsibility, and jurisdictional requirements.

### Jurisdictional implementation

- [Texas DPS CJIS Policy Components and Documents](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/crime-records/cjis-documents)
- The applicable state, tribal, territorial, federal, or local CJIS authority for the assessed system

These establish how a specific jurisdiction applies versions, audits, supplements, agreements, and evidence expectations. A Texas transition date is evidence about Texas. It is not silently promoted into a national rule.

## Current baseline note

The local source review used the 473-page FBI CJIS Security Policy v6.1 and the 86-page FBI Requirements Companion v6.1, both dated June 25, 2026, mirrored by Texas DPS. The FBI-hosted companion was also read through the FBI resource center. Representative companion pages were rendered to confirm the table structure, including:

- v6.0 and v6.1 control locations;
- title and “shall” statement;
- audit/sanction date;
- priority;
- IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS responsibility.

The companion states that the agency remains ultimately accountable even where the provider has technical responsibility.

## Maintenance rule

Before relying on this repository for a new assessment:

1. Check the FBI resource center for a newer policy and companion.
2. Confirm the current audit baseline and supplements with the applicable authority.
3. Record the confirmation date in the System Assessment.
4. Mark earlier conclusions `STALE` when a material governing fact changes.

Sources move. Authority changes. The repository should notice.
