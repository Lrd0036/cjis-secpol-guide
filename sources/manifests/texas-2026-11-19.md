Use the [Texas guide](../../docs/states/TEXAS.md) for applicability and the [state search data](../../data/state-search-pilot.json) for individual provisions, section locators and open questions. ## Changes since the September collection

- **One replacement among 14 DPS downloads.** Each item on the September manifest was retrieved through the November DPS listing and compared by SHA-256. Thirteen matched, including both FBI editions, their companions, the Texas supplement, contractor addendum, technical audit and incident form. DPS links to a different personnel chart. The listing still describes the March 31 revision while the file itself reads October 13, 2026. The October chart expressly includes active TCOLE communications and corrections officer licenses as well as peace officers. Its family-violence row expressly addresses convictions. It does not specify what governs every disposition omitted from that row. [Current listing](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/crime-records/cjis-documents) · [October chart](../texas/2026-11-19/system-access-policy.pdf) · [March chart](../texas/reference/system-access-chart.pdf)
- **Version labels have distinct uses.** The Texas audit notice specifies v5.9.5 through March 31, 2027 and v6.1 for gap assessments. The contractor packet contains v6.1 pages, the incident office cites v6.0 for its one-hour direction, and §VI.A of the Secure Site policy references v5.9.4. The records cite the source that actually governs the question being asked. [DPS audit edition](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/crime-records/cjis-documents) · [DPS incident office](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/crime-records/welcome-tx-cjis-security-office) · [CRD Secure Site policy](https://crimerecords.dps.texas.gov/DpsWebsite/CriminalHistory/SecurityPolicy.aspx)
- **The agency responsible for state cyber reporting changed.** Government Code §2063.302 and the current Texas Cyber Command intake are archived below. The June 26 Texas Register notice and its crosswalk move the information security rules to 1 TAC Chapter 477 effective August 1, 2026. TX-RAMP manual 4.0 took effect February 12 and uses earlier citations. [Chapter 2063](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/GV/htm/GV.2063.htm#2063.302) · [Rule transfer table](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/archive/June262026/Transferred%20Rules/202602469-1.pdf)
- **Training conflicts.** The live DPS Secure Site instructions require training before access and annually. The earlier standalone policy still uses six months and two years. OCA's live PSRS role table resolves inconsistent intervals in its May guide by distinguishing annual awareness from biennial mobile-access certification for users who run criminal-history queries. [DPS live policy](https://crimerecords.dps.texas.gov/DpsWebsite/CriminalHistory/SecurityPolicy.aspx) · [OCA live table](https://www.txcourts.gov/bail/mandatory-training-requirements/)
- **Dated local evidence.** The Bexar County Sheriff's page describes its own Flock records. San Marcos gives a termination date for its city-contracted cameras. Houston's vendor instructions apply to HPD access. These three sources are indexed with their locality. [Bexar County](https://www.bexar.org/4072/Flock-Safety) · [San Marcos](https://www.sanmarcostx.gov/4530/Automated-License-Plate-Readers-ALPR) · [Houston](https://www.houstontx.gov/police/cjis/hpdvendorcertification.htm)
