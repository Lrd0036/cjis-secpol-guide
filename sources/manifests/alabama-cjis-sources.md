## Access boundary

ALEA's operational CJIC manuals are exposed through the authenticated AlaCOP manuals portal at <https://cjicmanuals.alacop.gov/>. The public landing page requires a login, so no manuals were downloaded or inferred from that system. This is a real evidence gap: an Alabama assessment may require rules, agreements, training material, or agency-specific direction available only to authorized users.

The manifest also excludes unrelated documents merely hosted by ALEA or mentioning CJIS, including forfeiture reports, procurement solicitations, annual reports, crime-statistics publications, and the excessive-force complaint form. Those documents may matter to a different inquiry, but they do not define the baseline security/compliance source set collected here.
