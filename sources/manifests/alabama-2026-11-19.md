## What the captures establish

- **Published rules.** The Administrative Code's public `publicCode` operation was queried with commission number `265` and `asOnDate=2026-11-19`. The result lists 12 chapters and 87 rule or appendix entries. Each chapter PDF was retrieved from the publisher's chapter endpoint. Historical effective dates vary by rule. All twelve November PDF digests differ from September. For each chapter, the sequence of words and numbers extracted with pdftotext matched after normalizing case and Unicode and removing running headers, footers, whitespace and punctuation. This comparison checks the extractable text. It does not compare form images or typography. [Publisher index](https://admincode.legislature.state.al.us/administrative-code/265) · [Dated response](../alabama/2026-11-19/commission-index.json)
- **Article 23.** The Legislature's public `divisions` and `sections` queries returned all three current divisions of article `1507`. Their division IDs are `1510`, `1582` and `51614`. The response contains 56 distinct section rows (15, 37 and 4 respectively), including five repealed sections. Division 3 contains the forfeiture reporting law. The September response requested the first two divisions and included 55 version rows across 52 distinct sections. November rows carry null effective-date and supersession fields, so the Act and rule histories supply the cited effective dates. [Public Legislature API](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/graphql) · [Full November response](../alabama/2026-11-19/article-23.json) · [September response](../alabama/statutes/code-of-alabama-article-23-divisions-1-2.json)
- **Related Code.** A separate current-text request to the same Legislature API captured 23 sections. These are §§8-38-1 through 8-38-12 (breach notification), 11-5-31 (coroners), 12-15-133–134 (juvenile records), 15-27-6, -7 and -16 (expungement), 16-22A-5 (education screening), 36-12-40 (public records), 38-13-3–4 (care settings), and 41-27-5 (ALEA authority). The saved responses retain the publisher's HTML text, section IDs and history. Search records link to individual sections on the public Code site. [Related-sections response](../alabama/2026-11-19/related-code-sections.json)
- **October law change.** Act 2026-374 §1 amended §§41-9-590, -594, -625 and -650. Section 2 added §§41-9-650.1 through .5. Section 3 repealed Article 4 of Chapter 2, Title 32. Section 4 set October 1, 2026 as the effective date. The November Code display for §650 carries an amendment notice and the superseded text. In the enrolled PDF, read the deletion and insertion on printed pp. 10–11 (PDF pp. 11–12). This discrepancy has a note on the corresponding search record. [Code §41-9-650](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/code-of-alabama?section=41-9-650) · [Act, revised text](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/files/pdf/SearchableInstruments/2026RS/SB118-enr.pdf#page=11) · [Repeal and effective date](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/files/pdf/SearchableInstruments/2026RS/SB118-enr.pdf#page=19)
- **Pending filing.** The August Administrative Monthly published notice of proposed Chapter 265-X-13, Agency Personnel Reporting. The comment deadline was October 16. The dated code index still ends at Chapter 12. The proposal file and the search watchlist preserve that distinction. [Official notice, August issue](https://admincode.legislature.state.al.us/api/aam/2026-08#page=27) · [Proposed chapter](https://admincode.legislature.state.al.us/api/filing/6a6b7ac3bf4a671e3ae84fd9/filing)
- **Records schedules.** The October 2026 local law enforcement schedule and the October 2024 ALEA schedule have different custodians. Use printed pp. 24 and 26 for the local series, and pp. 40–41 and 55–57 for ALEA's audit practice and disposition items. Archives URLs are its document download links. [Local schedule](https://archives.alabama.gov/RDA/?id=19) · [ALEA schedule](https://archives.alabama.gov/RDA/?id=146)
- **Personal access forms.** The Form 46 download filename contains `rev.2.2`, while its pages print rev. 2.1, March 8, 2023. ALEA's Criminal Records and Alabama Background Check pages link, respectively, to older `_1` and `NEW_16` forms. Both older files are identified in the September register. The personal-review form, an employer's authorization and the security agreement for a vendor maintaining LPR data serve different purposes.
